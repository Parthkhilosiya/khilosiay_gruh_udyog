About Us

Welcome to Khilosiya Gruh Udyog!

At " Khilosiya Gruh Udyog ", we pride ourselves on bringing you the best of traditional Indian sweets and namkeen, delivered straight to your doorstep. Our journey began with a simple mission: to share the joy of authentic, homemade flavors with everyone.

Our Story

Founded in 2002, Khilosiya Gruh Udyog started as a small home-based business driven by a passion for creating delicious and high-quality snacks. Over the years, we've grown into a beloved brand that customers trust for their festive celebrations and everyday cravings alike. Our roots in family recipes and dedication to traditional methods ensure that every bite is a delightful experience.

What We Offer
  • Sweets: Ladoos, Barfis, Pedas, and more
  • Namkeen: Sev, Bhujia, Mixtures, and much more
  • Festive Specials: Customized gift boxes for all occasions
Our Commitment

At " Khilosiya Gruh Udyog ", quality is our top priority. We follow stringent hygiene practices and use only the best ingredients to ensure that our products not only taste great but are also safe and healthy. We believe in delivering freshness, which is why all our products are made to order and shipped promptly.

Why Choose Us?
  • Authentic Flavors: Time-honored recipes and traditional methods.
  • Premium Quality: Fresh, high-quality ingredients with no preservatives.
  • Convenient Shopping: Easy online ordering with fast, reliable delivery.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Our customers' happiness is our ultimate goal.
Join Our Family

We invite you to explore our range of sweets and namkeen and experience the taste of tradition with Khilosiya Gruh Udyog. Whether you're celebrating a special occasion or simply indulging in a treat, we're here to make every moment sweeter and more enjoyable.
